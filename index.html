Meet Miles

A self-taught barber who built the craft cut by cut.

Miles says he started by cutting his own hair, then his brother’s and neighborhood kids’ hair before working in a barbershop after high school. Today, his Booksy profile lists him as a licensed barber with 17 years of experience.

In a 2024 interview, he said he enjoys bald/skin fades for the light-to-dark blend and values the people and networking that come with barbering.
